# 📚 Learning repos & academies to mine

**The fastest way into GTM engineering is to clone what already works.** Five repos, three free vendor academies, a community and a newsletter cover most of the on-ramp. Mine the repos for *both* structure and content (with license discipline), earn the free certs to speak the vendors' vocabulary, and read practitioners to see the real questions hiring managers ask.

*Last checked: 17 Nov 2026. Star counts and licences come from the GitHub API on that date.*

> [!TIP]
> License discipline matters if you plan to publish your own repo: **MIT / Apache / CC0 → adapt with attribution** (`License · Source · Authors`); **CC-BY → adapt with attribution;** **CC-BY-SA → link + commentary only, never copy verbatim.** Not every repo below is MIT. Check the licence column before you copy anything. ⚠️ marks a licence that is missing, unclear or restricts use.

> [!NOTE]
> **Vendor certs are vocabulary, not proof of skill.** They teach you the tool's mental model and the words hiring managers use — worth the weekend. But a cert is not a portfolio; it's the [projects](07-portfolio-projects.md) that show you can build. Earn the cert to read the JD, then ship the repo to answer it.
