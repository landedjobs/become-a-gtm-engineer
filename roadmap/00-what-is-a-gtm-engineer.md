# 🧭 What is a GTM Engineer?

**GTM Engineering is what go-to-market looks like when it turns into software.** Clay says its founders coined the title in 2023 for an internal role, and that early employee Yash Tekriwal was the first to hold it ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, 10 Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer), official). Clay's working definition: GTM engineers *"increase revenue efficiency by combining creative GTM thinking with the ability to build at scale."* Its April 2026 guide says the role has since appeared at Cursor, Lovable and Webflow, and counts about 100 new GTME job listings a month ([Clay blog, 21 Apr 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering), official, Clay's own count). You are not "doing marketing ops with better tools". You build the enrichment, scoring, routing, CRM write-back and AI agents the revenue team runs on.

Expect to be asked where your role ends and RevOps, sales engineering or growth engineering begins. Get this wrong and you sound like a Zapier power-user or a backend engineer who has never met a sales floor.

*Last checked: 17 Nov 2026. Evidence labels: **official** = the company's own page or posting; **first-person** = a practitioner's own account; **second-hand** = an article, recruiter report or aggregator.*

## The builder vs conductor vs translator map

These labels are a study aid, not an industry standard. Each cell is backed by a page you can open. A fourth lens, the growth engineer, is added because the title overlaps more every quarter.

| Dimension | **GTM Engineer** (builder) | **RevOps** (conductor) | **Sales Engineer** (translator) | **Growth Engineer** (experimenter) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Defining focus | Builds automated revenue systems with AI, enrichment and workflow automation ([Clay, Apr 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering)) | Owns data pipelines and data quality, which is why most companies start GTMEs inside it ([Clay, Apr 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering)) | Bridges a prospect's technical needs and a complex product to close the deal ([Salesforce, May 2024](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/what-is-a-sales-engineer/)) | Builds and A/B tests growth product experiments ([HyperGrowth Partners, Nov 2024](https://playbooks.hypergrowthpartners.com/p/the-rise-of-the-gtm-engineer)) |
| Typical work product | Inbound scoring and routing, enrichment, rep-facing tools ([Clay Marketing posting, Oct 2026](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/9f8067d1-61cf-42cd-aad7-0ac54de8b345)) | Territory planning and post-sale account assignment ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)) | Product demonstrations and plain-language technical explanation ([Salesforce, May 2024](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/what-is-a-sales-engineer/)) | Pricing-page, signup and onboarding experiments ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-growth-engineer)) |
| Measured on | Pipeline created, stage-one-to-stage-two conversion, quota capacity per seller (Clay's internal GTMEs, Q3 2026, [official](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)) | Not one metric in our sources; Clay frames it as owning data quality | Not one metric in our sources; the job is helping a specific customer buy | Product metrics such as clickthrough, activation and signups ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-growth-engineer)) |

> [!NOTE]
> **They are complementary, and the lines are moving.** Clay says RevOps and GTM engineering are "coming closer together" and that it plans to merge them further. Its CX GTME already does both: next-best-action agents plus territory planning ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, 10 Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer), official). In an interview, name which lens you're using (*build, integrate, or operate*) so you're graded against the right rubric.

### Clay's two tracks: the same title on two different jobs

Clay's own careers board shows why the title alone tells you little (all **official**, opened 17 Nov 2026):

- **Internal GTMEs** sit in the *Revenue Platform* department, *GTM Operations* team. Live postings: [GTM Engineer - Marketing](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/9f8067d1-61cf-42cd-aad7-0ac54de8b345) (posted 12 Oct 2026), [GTM Engineer - Systems and Infrastructure](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/f90028b7-6c35-4392-824c-105967ccd406) (9 Jun 2026), [GTM Engineer - CX](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/7d362dfe-02b5-48a6-bce3-67f9de9724d0) (3 Nov 2026) and a [GTME Manager, Seller Efficiency](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/546b27c1-8808-4808-bd98-5c6cad88d88c) (31 Aug 2026). Clay splits these into two archetypes: *systems and infrastructure* GTMEs, who own the data model and integrations, and *functional* GTMEs, aligned to one business team ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, 10 Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)).
- **Customer-facing GTMEs** sit in the *GTM - Sales* department. Roles such as [Account Executive (GTME - New Business)](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/claylabs/a6abd176-8afe-4905-a341-15d89f9acd03) (posted 12 Aug 2026) open under the heading "GTM Engineer @ Clay" but describe quota-carrying account executives who run consultative discovery and demos. An earlier customer-facing "GTM Engineer - Sales Led" posting no longer loads (404 on 17 Nov 2026).

Revnu's read of 131 LinkedIn postings (second-hand, published 2 Jun 2026) found the same split market-wide: roughly 65% internal builders, 25% customer-facing roles close to solutions engineering, and 10% RevOps analyst roles with a new title ([Revnu](https://revnu.partners/blog/gtm-engineer-hiring)). Read the department, the reporting line and the success metrics before you apply.

### The growth-engineer overlap

HyperGrowth Partners (Nov 2024, second-hand) separates the two: growth engineers ship product experiments, while GTM engineers are "not confined to the product itself" and also run the SaaS stack and data pipelines ([HyperGrowth](https://playbooks.hypergrowthpartners.com/p/the-rise-of-the-gtm-engineer)). PostHog's growth-engineering examples are product work, such as a pricing call-to-action change that lifted clickthroughs 27% ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-growth-engineer), official for PostHog's own results).

Live postings blur this (official, opened 17 Nov 2026):

- Perplexity's [Member of Technical Staff (GTM Engineer, Growth)](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Perplexity/3b523d3a-23bd-4887-8e3c-239d14d66b06) (posted 4 Nov 2026) sits on the Growth team, ships in-product paths from self-serve to sales and runs A/B experiments. Perplexity's other [Growth GTM Engineer, Revenue Operations](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Perplexity/e916f0c8-5a31-4bb6-b4e3-7c4a64b95fda) (13 Nov 2026) owns enrichment, scoring and routing and reports to the Head of Marketing Operations. Same company, same month, two different jobs.
- AgentMail's [GTM Engineer](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/AgentMail/0f25ff04-0250-4c8c-b9a6-00b164f1f19a) (13 Jul 2026) sits in Growth and owns the growth data layer, outbound and PLG experiments.

**Takeaway:** ask "Am I building the company's GTM system, helping customers buy and implement, or changing the product's funnel?" Treat "GTM engineer", "growth engineer" and "revenue engineer" as search terms, not as job descriptions.

## Four pillars of the AI GTM Engineer

No single source owns this list. These four responsibilities recur across the live postings we opened on 17 Nov 2026. If a job description lists them, it is this role whatever the title says:

1. **AI assistant & workflow development.** Build the agents and automations that do revenue work. Baseten wants someone to "ship AI-powered workflows for the field" ([Baseten, Aug 2026](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/baseten/5cd2f489-b9ee-428b-b252-94e83d55f107)); Intercom's GTM engineers build agents for account research, call prep and renewal risk ([Intercom/Fin, Mar 2026](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/intercom/jobs/7749323)).
2. **AI tool setup & optimization.** Stand up and tune the stack. AgentMail's GTM engineer decides "what tools we use and what gets kicked to the curb" ([AgentMail, Jul 2026](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/AgentMail/0f25ff04-0250-4c8c-b9a6-00b164f1f19a)); Baseten asks you to audit the stack and build your own backlog.
3. **Team enablement & training.** Make reps and marketers use what you built. Intercom's role writes the playbook and runs the enablement session; Vercel's [GTM Engineer](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/vercel/jobs/6225825004) (Oct 2026) publishes guides so other teams can build their own tools.
4. **GTM automation strategy.** Decide *what* to automate. At Clay, work that doesn't improve pipeline generation, pipeline efficiency or expansion goes to the bottom of the list ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)); Secfix's first GTM engineer decides with the Head of GTM what to build next ([Secfix, Nov 2026](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/secfix/59670476-4f25-48a3-a11e-66c7a72416bf)).

All of the above are **official** job postings, live on 17 Nov 2026.

## The T-shaped skillset

HyperGrowth Partners (Nov 2024) calls the GTM engineer's profile **T-shaped**: ICP, messaging and customer-journey knowledge across the top, plus business modelling and semi-technical skill in coding, SaaS, data and APIs ([HyperGrowth](https://playbooks.hypergrowthpartners.com/p/the-rise-of-the-gtm-engineer)). Most holders did not start as software engineers: in Kenso's profile study, 7.7% of new GTM engineers came straight from software or data engineering, with sales, marketing, ops and founder roles each supplying more (six English-speaking markets, profiles up to March 2026, [Kenso Research](https://ken.so/research/rise-of-the-gtm-engineer.pdf), second-hand). Clay says GTMEs do "not necessarily" need to code ([Clay, Apr 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering)), but 12 of the 15 postings in our [Nov 2026 cross-section](06-tools-and-stack.md#what-postings-actually-ask-for-nov-2026) name Python or SQL. You need:

- **APIs & webhooks**: the integration spine (OAuth, HMAC, idempotency, rate limits). 14 of 15 postings name APIs.
- **SQL**: to model the funnel and score accounts in the warehouse. 12 of 15.
- **LLM structured outputs & tool calls**: the AI layer. 12 of 15 postings ask for agent or LLM-tool work.
- **A CRM data model in your head**: Account/Contact/Opportunity and where the join lives. 10 of 15 name Salesforce.
- **Commercial instinct**: tie every build to a pipeline number. Perplexity's RevOps posting asks you to start from a revenue problem and work back to the fix.

## How real teams structure the role

Named companies *diverge* on the org chart, and that divergence is itself a senior talking point. *Last checked: 17 Nov 2026.*

**How the title spread.** The counts below use different methods, so treat them as separate signals, not one trend line:

- **Clay's account (official):** coined in 2023 for an internal role, then spread to customers and competitors; about 100 new listings a month in April 2026 ([Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering); [The GTM Engineer by Clay](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)).
- **Pave payroll data, as cited by Clay (second-hand):** companies with a GTME on payroll rose from under 0.1% in January 2023 to 3.2% in September 2026 ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, 10 Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)).
- **Kenso profile study (second-hand):** people holding a core GTM engineer title grew 6.5x, from 121 at the end of 2023 to 791 in March 2026. It covers public profiles in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand only, and misses people doing the work under other titles ([Kenso Research](https://ken.so/research/rise-of-the-gtm-engineer.pdf)).
- **Onfire (second-hand, 31 Aug 2026):** about 1,200 people on LinkedIn held the title in May 2026, and 91% of companies with a GTM engineer have only one, citing an earlier review of 1,000+ profiles ([Onfire](https://www.onfire.ai/blog/gtm-engineering-data)).

> [!TIP]
> The shared pattern under the divergence: **several of these teams separate fast *pilots* from slow, governed *production* systems** (Intercom's GTM Ops vs GTM Systems is the clearest case). Clay's own advice is to "start in ops, federate later": incubate GTM engineering under RevOps, then move people out once the data is clean ([Clay, Apr 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/gtm-engineering)). Clay also says no funding stage triggers the first hire; product-market fit and a motion worth systemising do ([The GTM Engineer by Clay, Nov 2026](https://thegtme.com/p/how-to-hire-a-go-to-market-engineer)). Our view: copying Notion's three-team split before you have plays to coordinate adds overhead with nothing to coordinate.

## The interview angle you'll be graded on first

Clay's [hiring guide](https://www.clay.com/blog/how-to-hire-a-gtm-engineer) (Apr 2026, official) lists **commercial bias** as a trait to screen for: *"automation should drive revenue outcomes, not just efficiency"*. A candidate who says *"I'd wire up Clay and Outreach to save the team time"* reads as posturing. The strong opening: **name the funnel stage with the worst conversion and the unit economic you'd move, then reach for the tool.** Technical elegance with no revenue attached is the answer that ends the round.
