# 💰 GTM engineer salaries in 2026: US, Europe, India

*Last checked: 17 Nov 2026*

**Short answer.** In the US, the median posted base for GTM engineering roles is about **$160K**. Four separate posting analyses land within $500 of that figure. The UK, the rest of Europe and India pay much less, and the spread inside each region is wide. In India it depends mostly on the kind of employer. No single band covers the role, so this page lists each source with what it actually measures.

Every figure below says what it covers: base or OTE, level, location, sample size, date and source type. Sources are ordered by strength. First come actual postings with a stated range. Then posting analyses and aggregators. Then recruiter bands.

**Source types used on this page**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Official** | The employer's own posting, on its careers page or ATS. |
| **Job-board copy** | A third-party board reposting a job. Second-hand: the original may differ or be closed. |
| **Aggregator** | A company that collects many postings and computes medians. Second-hand. Check its sample and method. |
| **Recruiter band** | A recruiting firm's guidance from its own placements. Second-hand. Useful context, not a census. |

### Where US sources disagree

- **The center agrees. The spread does not.** All four analyses put the median at $159.5K–$160K. Onfire reports US ranges from $60K to $250K. GTM Engineer Search's middle half is $135K–$185K. AutoInterviewAI's 11 postings run $115K–$215K.
- **A single posting is not the market.** Conduit's $180K–$240K base sits above the middle half of the 947-posting sample. Use it as one employer's offer, not as a typical band.
- **Lead or manager ($160K) sits below Staff ($200K)** in the GTM Engineer Search cut. Different companies use these titles, so the order is not a promotion path.

## 📖 How to read salary data

- **Check the pay basis first.** Base is fixed salary. OTE (on-target earnings) adds variable pay at target. Indian CTC (cost to company) can include variable pay, allowances and employer contributions. A $160K base and a $160K OTE are different offers.
- **Midpoint medians are not offers.** Most analyses count each posted range at its midpoint. The median of midpoints tells you where postings center, not what any one person was paid.
- **Look at the sample.** 947 postings and 3 postings are not the same kind of evidence. Small samples show a direction, not a band.
- **Job-board copies go stale.** Prefer the employer's own posting. A reposted range may be closed or edited.
- **Recruiter bands describe a recruiter's candidates.** They are often higher than public postings, and their method is not published.
- **Match the population.** Compare roles of the same level, location, employer type and employment status (employee or contractor).
- LPA means lakh per annum. 1 lakh is 100,000 rupees.

## 📈 Equity and variable pay

- **Equity is common at venture-backed employers, and the terms vary.** Conduit states 0.20%–0.75%. Clay and Baseten mark their postings "Offers Equity" without a size. Secfix offers shares. None of these gives a dollar value, so ask for the grant size, strike price, vesting and the latest valuation.
- **Variable pay is stated less often than base.** Scooter posts INR 5–10 lakh variable on top of base. Checkpoint GTM ties its whole range to billings and sales. BisonRS says a bonus of 10–20% of base is typical in the UK and Europe (recruiter view, Aug 2026).
- **Medians leave variable pay out.** GTM Engineer Search excluded 91 USD pay disclosures because they were OTE or total cash. If an offer is quoted as OTE, ask for the base, the target variable and how payout is measured.
